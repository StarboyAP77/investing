📏 Guyton-Klinger Guardrails

Starting withdrawal percentage of portfolio (annualized). If current withdrawal rate exceeds this, decrease withdrawal. If current withdrawal rate falls below this, increase withdrawal. Percentage to adjust withdrawal when a guardrail is breached. Amount to skim into Bunker when withdrawal rate falls below lower guardrail (portfolio is healthy).
Prevents increases after loss years and decreases after gain years.

🌾 Volatility Harvesting (Shannon's Demon)

What is this? Markets zig and zag. Volatility Harvesting is the mechanical process of skimming profits from the asset that "zigged" and injecting it into the asset that "zagged."
Why does it matter? It forces you to "Buy Low and Sell High" mathematically. It proves if Rebalancing is actually making you extra money (Positive Alpha) or just costing you momentum (Negative Alpha).
